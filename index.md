# Privacy Policy for Spiders:Burn them out

**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

**Rubarion Games** ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to the virtual reality application **Spiders:Burn them out** (the "Application").

### 1. Information Collection and Use
The Application is designed as a standalone offline experience. We **do not collect, store, process, or share** any personal data, location data, biometric data, device identifiers, or usage analytics from your device or Meta Horizon account.

### 2. Internet Connectivity & Third-Party Services
The Application operates completely offline and does not connect to external servers or utilize third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

### 3. Data Storage
All game save files and local settings are stored exclusively on your Meta Quest device. We do not have access to these files.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Because our Application does not collect any personal information, it strictly complies with applicable children's privacy regulations (including COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

### 5. Data Deletion & User Rights
Since we do not collect or store any user data on external servers, there is no personal data to view, modify, or delete. Uninstalling the Application from your Meta Quest headset will permanently delete all locally stored save files.
